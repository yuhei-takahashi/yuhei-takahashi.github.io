Yuhei Takahashi is currently pursuing a Ph.D. degree in the Department of Intelligent Information Engineering and Sciences, Doshisha University, Kyoto, Japan.

He received the B.S. and M.S. degrees from Doshisha University in 2021 and 2023, respectively. Since April 2023, he has been working as a communication systems engineer with Hitachi, Ltd., Japan.

His research interests include random access protocols, coded slotted ALOHA, multi-antenna reception, coding theory, successive interference cancellation, and radio communication systems.
